Riga, 2025

variable typeface, zine, animation

Riga is an experimental graffiti-style typeface that was created over the course of four months by collecting and documenting graffiti in the streets around me during my stay in Riga as part of the Erasmus+ program. At first, I was just documenting, but the more I walked through the city, the more the project became something personal – a way to connect not only with the new city but also with its rhythm, chaos, voice, and traces of hundreds of anonymous citizen. This typeface is a way to capture these traces and transfer them into adifferent context where they can be re–read and rewritten.
