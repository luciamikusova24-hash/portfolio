Narkokapitalismus

concept of book design, 2023

Book typesetting, layout, and selection of typography and color scheme. The visual concept is based on the aesthetics of medical prescriptions: blue text, a simple structure, and a cool, detached tone. The cover is minimalist, featuring a hand written text, that resembles doctor’s often unreadable handwriting. The same visual system works inside of the book with blue text and handwritten chapters.
