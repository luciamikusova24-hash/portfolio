Gracia castle Solčany

logo, typography, color palette, supporting visual system for both print and digital use

A visual identity for a historic manor house in the village of Solčany, which is undergoing renovation and conversion into affordable housing and a community center. The logo is based on an elegant serif typeface and draws on the contrast between tradition and modernity. The emphasis is on dignity, tranquility, and balance, which reflect both the architecture of the site and its new social purpose. The color palette is soft and earthy, evoking timelessness, light, and local character.

Created at Wink&Nod digital agency.
