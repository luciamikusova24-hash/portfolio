✿flowerfields✿

visuals and sound for a fictional podcast, 2023

Flower Fields is a fictional podcast focused on experimenting with poetry and sound. As part of the project, a concept for four episodes was developed, one of which was written, recorded, and produced as a preview of the format and atmosphere of the entire series.

Part of the concept is a simple website design as well as short video/ animation for the first episode. Another part of the project is a zine, using stills from the video itself and poetry.
